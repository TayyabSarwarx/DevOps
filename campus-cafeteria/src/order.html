How to Order

Choose your favourite food items from the menu and visit the Campus Bites counter to place your order.

Order Steps

  1. Select your food items from the menu.
  2. Place your order at the Campus Bites counter.
  3. Pay at the counter.
  4. Collect your order token.
  5. Wait for your token number to be announced, then collect your food.

Available Items

Fresh snacks, burgers, sandwiches and drinks.

Order Timings

Available during cafeteria opening hours.
